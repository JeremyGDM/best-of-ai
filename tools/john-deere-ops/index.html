// about John Deere Operations Center

John Deere Operations Center is the digital hub for precision agriculture on John Deere equipment, aggregating machine data, agronomic inputs, and field maps in one place. AI-powered prescriptions recommend variable-rate planting and application strategies field by field. It's the cornerstone of John Deere's connected agriculture ecosystem, linking the office to the cab in real time.
