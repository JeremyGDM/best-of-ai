// about OpenAI Operator

Operator is OpenAI's AI agent that can browse the web and complete multi-step tasks on your behalf — booking restaurants, filling out forms, shopping, researching, and more. It uses a vision-based approach to interact with websites as a human would, without requiring sites to have a specific API. Launched in early 2025, Operator represents OpenAI's push into the agentic AI space where AI doesn't just answer questions but actively accomplishes goals. It is available to ChatGPT Pro subscribers and being expanded to other tiers.
