// about Durable

Durable is an AI website builder that creates a complete, designed website for a small business in under 30 seconds from a short description. It generates copy, selects images, and structures pages tailored to the business type, then lets you edit everything with a chat interface. Freelancers and local businesses use it to have a professional online presence without any design or coding skills.
